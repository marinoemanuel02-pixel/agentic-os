# Kling recipe

Kling's terms forbid driving it: **write a prompt sheet for the operator, never automate it.** Terms of Service at
kling.ai/docs/user-policy (updated 2026-04-21, read 2026-11-13), section 3.1(h): users may not "use automated
scripts to collect information from or otherwise interact with the Services". Section 4.6 restricts use of Output
without written permission. Kling now lives at kling.ai (it was app.klingai.com on 2026-11-12).

## Prompt sheet

Put the two keyframes (1280x720 PNG) and a PROMPT-SHEET.md in the film's `generated/kling/`: the mode (Generate,
video, reference-mode dropdown: Keyframes), the first and last frame files, 16:9, 720p, 5 s, the model (the flagship
cost 50 credits for a 720p 5 s take on 2026-11-12, the fast one 30), the prompt, and where to save the mp4 (with its
watermark). Until the take arrives the film can carry a stand-in from another platform in the same shot.

## Observed on 2026-11-12 (before the terms were read; kept as reference for the operator)

- Credits in the left rail (66 on a fresh free account). Keyframes mode shows First frame and Last frame slots; a drop
  on the strip adds a Middle frame instead, so use the Last frame tile's Upload.
- The free queue took about 4 minutes. Output: 1280x720, 24 fps, 5 s, audio track, a visible "KlingAI" watermark.
- Particles moved into the end keyframe (reached, not faded in); the disc looked stiff and the burst started within
  3 frames.
