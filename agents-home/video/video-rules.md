# Video rules (read at every stage; `aos video preflight` lists them; ids never change: a new rule takes the next free id and goes under its stage)

## Brief

- R01 [manual] Never start production before the definition of good, a style frame and an animated hero shot exist and have been reviewed (by the user when they gate, by the director at playback speed when they do not).  (from agi-race 2026-09-29)
- R09 [manual] Research: open the page for every claim in the script; search-result summaries are leads, not sources.  (from agi-race-2 2026-10-01)
- R19 [manual] Easy to follow: one idea per line in everyday words, cases counted aloud, the picture shows the idea before the words name it, few words on screen.  (from the alignment-3 brief 2026-10-02)
- R27 [check: currency] Fast-moving topics need a currency gate before the script: a dated research brief from primary pages (source date, retrieval date and version such as arXiv vN), items tagged fact, measurement, opinion, forecast or contested, "as of" on screen, and every item re-verified on export day. A source's version is part of the citation: the same URL gave a different level name two years apart.  (from the v4 verdict and the Morris et al. 2023/2025 finding, 2026-10-03) Gate (build and QA refuse a film without currency.json unless qa.env declares CURRENCY=none for a timeless topic): tools/checks/currency.mjs refuses an empty ledger, a missing field, a stale retrieval, a too-old source, an unmapped factual/quoted claim or a contested item without a counter-voice from another camp; on export day tools/reverify.mjs re-opens every page and checks its anchor (browser-only pages are confirmed by hand), and both build and QA pass --claims and --export, which requires a successful re-verification dated on the actual local export day. The input manifest binds currency.json before rendering; changing it after sealing requires a rebuild.
- R39 [manual] Source each shot by need, in this order: the subject's own material (its paper, launch or demo video, live page capture), coverage excerpts (news, other creators), open libraries (stock, CC0 models, HDRIs, sound), then built. shots.md records the need and the source; the brief establishes the channel's policy and the approved download budget, and sourcing.json records them for the tools.  (from the 2026-10-06 request)

## Look

- R03 [manual] No generic generated imagery as the default: generative engines are opt-in and must pass the anti-slop gate. Imported real material (clips, photos, page captures, scanned models) is allowed when it shows its line's specific idea and is graded into the design system (R35, R39). Permission to import is not permission to mix media: R28 decides how practices mix.  (from agi-race 2026-09-29; real material allowed 2026-10-06)
- R04 [manual] Anti-slop gate: reject a generic stock or generic generated look, visuals that do not carry their line, AI artifacts, style drift, motion that goes nowhere (each one alone fails the shot).  (from agi-race 2026-09-29; generated imagery added 2026-10-06, the criteria stay independent)
- R05 [manual] The voice is part of the style lock: send a short audition of 3-4 voices with the style frame, because no agent can judge a voice by ear; never ship a voice the user has not heard without saying so.  (from agi-race-2 2026-10-01)
- R13 [manual] Authored asset files are only a starting point for craft. Check silhouette, surface, articulated contact, readable framing and visual meaning in the rendered result.  (from alignment v2 2026-10-02)
- R17 [check: motion] Every move goes through src/lib/motion.ts; the motion check reports snaps in the export and expo curves in the source, and every hit is inspected. Motion targets live in frontier.md.
- R18 [manual] Craft targets live in agents-home/video/frontier.md, one entry per dimension: start from each best exemplar and beat it in the look lab. No rule here limits the medium; the brief chooses it.
- R28 [manual] Several visualization practices in one film need a design system (palette, type, grid, recurring motif, transition and sound language) proven by a hero that holds two practices; choose practices in a look lab (same beats, 3 practices, quarter scale) scored blind on frames by both vendors.

## Structure

- R02 [manual] No slideshow pacing: every shot has motion inside it and a purpose; cuts land on the beat.  (from agi-race 2026-09-29)
- R06 [manual] Freeze line slots in beats once picture work starts; a new voice must fit the slots, not move the cuts.  (from agi-race-2 2026-10-01)
- R29 [check: pacing] Engagement is measured, not assumed: report shots per minute, the longest hold with no visual change, practice changes, the share of on-screen words not in the narration and time to the first hook, and compare with the last film. Dynamism comes from cut rhythm, variety, acting and sound; R17's calm easing stays.  (from the v4 verdict, 2026-10-03) Report: tools/checks/pacing.mjs (QA fails if it cannot write qa/pacing.md; set PACING_BASELINE to the last film's pacing.json for the comparison; the numbers are reported, reviewers judge). alignment-now vs v4: 6.9 vs 2.3 shots/min, 9 practices.

## Production

- R07 [manual] Review every delegated shot on a contact sheet (first, middle, last frame) before merging its look into the film; a passing verify only proves it renders.  (from agi-race-2 2026-10-01)
- R10 [manual] Validate media roots before any write and keep each version's rendered public assets separate; sharing a source or module junction must not share writable output.  (from alignment v2 2026-10-02)
- R12 [manual] Transition source scenes must omit captions that would shrink or be wiped into fragments; inspect first, middle and last transition frames at full resolution.  (from alignment v2 2026-10-02)
- R15 [manual] Set PYTHONUTF8=1 and PYTHONIOENCODING=utf-8 for media CLIs on Windows. Treat a transcript sentence over a verified silent voice tail as a recognizer error, not additional narration.  (from alignment v2 2026-10-02)
- R16 [manual] Serialize verification runs that share a bundle folder. Parallel inspection is safe; rebundling while another render reads that folder is not.  (from alignment v2 2026-10-02)
- R23 [manual] Dissolves join identical camera poses held throughout the overlap; cuts are exact match cuts or clear reframes. Run tools/checks/joins.mts before rendering and QA; shotCamera rejects non-finite, malformed or unordered keys. Camera drift and lighting use the film frame across a dissolve.  (from the AGI/ASI camera join and black-frame fixes 2026-10-03)
- R24 [check: screen-text] Every authored on-screen string has a reviewed claims.json screen entry; use literal text properties/attributes or JSX children in authored shot/library files. Keep forwarding helpers free of authored words. Coverage is mechanical; truth, clipping and overlapping text require independent source/frame review.  (from the AGI/ASI screen ledger 2026-10-03)
- R25 [manual] Word-timed picture and effects share cue-core; measure the existing local voice WAVs and record words.sha256.json. Refuse a changed voice before overwriting any stems. Use BEAT/FPS for the soundtrack grid and AOS_VIDEO_MEDIA_ROOT for every writable media tool, with private verifier bundles.  (from the AGI/ASI timing/private-root fixes 2026-10-03)
- R26 [manual] A build is sealed: never edit a hashed input, a script it runs (bash reads scripts while it runs) or the checkout during tools/build.sh and the QA that follows. Work that must continue (reviews, docs, the next brief) happens outside the hashed tree, or waits.  (from the AGI/ASI v1 build that died on an edited build.sh, 2026-10-03)
- R31 [manual] Every bug found on a render gets a guard at its source and, where code can detect it, a test that failed first. In the AGI/ASI run: a missing re-export became a NaN camera and a black frame (now a throw on non-finite or unordered camera keys), overlapping headlines (a throw), a stale voice hash (a build refusal, mutation-tested), an object popping into frame (found by the motion check, fixed in the object, no code guard). Fix the cause, not the frame; waive a motion hit only after full-size crops of its tile.  (from the AGI/ASI renders, 2026-10-03)
- R36 [manual] Where two boxes meet (shelves and panels, floors and sets), review three adjacent frames, not one still: a shimmer that changes frame to frame does not show in stills.  (from alignment-now v9 retro 44, 2026-10-06)

## QA

- R11 [manual] Run frozen-frame checks on the assembled timeline as well as suspect clips: a transition's held destination plus the following shot's quiet opening can fail across the cut. Never waive the detector for an unintended hold.  (from alignment v2 2026-10-02)
- R14 [manual] Distinguish reviewer evidence: a midpoint cannot establish that an answer never appears, a contact sheet cannot establish playback or sound, and unread sources cannot establish traceability. Preserve each independent verdict and its unknowns.  (from alignment v2 2026-10-02)
- R30 [manual] Independent reviews get images, early and at every gate: tools/review-sheets.mts makes the shot sheets (20/50/85 % of each shot) and the dissolve sheet; hand them to the other vendor with codex exec -i or an equivalent. A late review found what three own passes missed (source version drift, an unexplained mechanism). State what frames cannot establish (R14).  (from the AGI/ASI v3 REVISE review, 2026-10-03)
- R35 [check: assets] Every imported file has a row in assets.json: a basis (license from the allowlist, cc0, public-domain, fair-use, capture, own), rights evidence, an acquisition the film's sourcing.json accepts, a credit where its license asks for one, and derivatives whose hashes match. Fair use and captures: at most 8 s per use, 10 % of the film in total, sound muted unless it is the subject (5 s), narration over at least half of each use, a rationale and an on-screen source tag. Media reach the picture only through src/lib/media components and an asset id. The ledger starts at sourcing and the component rule applies in production; the gate (build and QA) checks policy, not law; qa/assets.md lists the human checks.  (from the asset-import spec, 2026-10-06)
- R37 [manual] Reviewer briefs ask "what would a first-time viewer not recognise or not know?" and "which sentence argues with the viewer?", not only "is it accurate and clean?".  (from alignment-now v9 retro 45, 2026-10-06)

## Delivery

- R22 [check: input-manifest] QA must bind the exact MP4 and captions to unchanged picture, public/audio assets, resolved fonts, config and runtime inputs, and reject mismatches before decoding or proxy rendering. The renderer src junction must resolve to the declared checkout.  (from the mixed alignment-3 export/proxy run and AGI/ASI guard regressions 2026-10-03)

## Orchestration

- R08 [manual] A tool or service is tested with one real call when it is added; a connector that needs credits is a paid dependency.  (from agi-race-2 2026-10-01)
- R20 [manual] Close the loop: read PREFLIGHT.md before the brief; record each stage's minutes with `aos video log` and every user verdict verbatim with `aos video verdict` (audit only); the retrospective turns each verdict into a kit change plus a rewritten frontier.md entry, and adds a rule here only for a technical or process guard, as a check rule with a script in tools/checks when a script can catch it.  (from alignment-3 2026-10-02; frontier 2026-10-26)
- R21 [manual] nar picks are hints: keep the call_id and the candidates, and send nar_feedback only when the user actually compares those candidates; a verdict on the finished film is not a label for an earlier choice.  (from the alignment-3 plan critique by Codex 2026-10-02)
- R32 [manual] Spend the machine, not the clock: keep the computer awake for long jobs, run reviews and docs (outside the hashed tree, R26) while a render runs, render review cuts at quarter scale, render only what changed, and log each stage's minutes. Measured once on this laptop, 180 heavy 3D frames (S12 of the AGI/ASI film) took 24-27 s at render concurrency 2, 3 and 4 and 29-59 s at 6-10 on an idle machine: wider did not help there, so keep the default 4 unless a re-measure on the new film says otherwise. A three-hour idle gap and four 15-minute full renders were the largest avoidable costs of the AGI/ASI run.  (from the AGI/ASI stage minutes and render timings, 2026-10-03) Render only what changed: tools/render-cached.sh (default in build.sh) keys each cut-to-cut segment on its transitive src/ imports plus the input manifest environment.
- R33 [manual] Shell hygiene on this machine: ffmpeg in a while-read loop needs -nostdin; run every media tool and the manifest verifier with the same AOS_VIDEO_MEDIA_ROOT; commit the config/places.json entry that aos video new writes (it dirties the shared checkout and blocks other sessions); never share one checkout between a builder and a reviewer session.  (from the AGI/ASI run, 2026-10-03)
- R34 [manual] Capacity is planned, not hoped for (`aos capacity` holds each vendor's last known window; `aos capacity set <vendor> <pct>` records what the usage card shows, a quota error in a delegate receipt records the reset time, and `aos delegate` warns before handing work to a limited or low vendor): at the start and at every gate read both vendors' limits (Claude usage, Codex's reset time from its last error or receipt), write the split (who builds what until when) in HANDOFF.md and an aos note, and when one vendor is limited the other takes the next batch at once in its own worktree, new files only. Never idle waiting for a reset; keep delegated runs lean (quarter-size stills, no repeated full renders) and give a detached delegate a wrapper limit above its own timeout.  (from alignment-now 2026-10-04: Codex hit its limit three times, Claude waited for it, and the user had to step in)
- R38 [manual] On OneDrive trees test old code with git archive <commit> | tar -x into a scratch folder, never git stash -u; git cannot delete folders there.  (from alignment-now retro 46 and the 2026-10-06 cleanup)
- R40 [manual] A delegated Codex run has a token budget (aos delegate --max-input, all input tokens counted) and a task file with a Read only: line naming the exact files and a Done = line naming the one check that proves it.  (from the 2026-10-06 runs: one delegate used 3.9 M input tokens and failed, another 3.5 M for a 20-35 K estimate)
- R41 [manual] Blender through the MCP: every set is a script in blender/ (s_<set>.py, SHOT variants) built on blender/lib.py; look-dev runs it live through the Blender MCP (one still per change, read back before the next), finals run the same file headless with tools/render-shot.py. Each MCP call is a fresh namespace and bpy.ops lose context after read_homefile: data API only. The add-on (Blender Lab "MCP", port 9876, autostart) lives in Blender's user extensions.  (from cant-escape-ai 2026-10-08: the earlier films used Blender without the MCP)
- R42 [manual] Not shapes, in 3D: scanned CC0 models and PBR textures (tools/polyhaven.py, logged against the film's BUDGET_MB), real public-domain imagery as textures (NASA Earth), HDRI light, practical lights, volumetric haze, shallow depth of field, motion blur; frame macro and medium shots of real objects; build geometry only where no scan exists, and then dress it with scanned material. Look at each new set at 35-50 % before rendering it.  (from cant-escape-ai 2026-10-08)
- R43 [manual] Measure render cost before batching: one warm frame per setting, written to renders/render-log.jsonl by render-shot. On the GTX 1660 Ti, EEVEE 1080x1920 cost 16 s/frame at 64 samples and 5.6 s at 24; 75 % scale with grain on top cut the film to about 1-3 s/frame. Volumetric tile 8 shows blocks; tile 2 only where spot shafts need it. Particles need a pre-roll (shots.json "preroll").  (from cant-escape-ai 2026-10-08)
- R44 [manual] Shorts cut on words, not on a music grid: tools/edit.py places measured lines and cuts each shot LEAD frames before the word that names its picture (src/data/cuts.json); target 2-3 s per shot, a hook in the first second, captions 84 px in the safe area with a scrim, the spoken word in the accent colour.  (from cant-escape-ai 2026-10-08)
- R45 [manual] Page captures never accept consent or close dialogs by clicking: tools/capture.mjs hides and removes overlays, records URL, time and SHA-256 in research/captures.json, and a headline card crops only the headline and its date.  (from cant-escape-ai 2026-10-08)
- R46 [manual] Queue renders in one command (or behind a lockfile), never behind a process-count wait: an open Blender GUI counts as a process and stalled the cant-escape-ai queue for 8 hours. At every wake-up read renders/render-log.jsonl and say how far the queue got.  (from cant-escape-ai 2026-10-08)
- R47 [manual] Read a mid-frame of every rendered clip before assembly, not only the look-dev stills: three shots whose camera was re-aimed after their last still came out nearly black in v1.  (from cant-escape-ai 2026-10-08)
- R48 [manual] Storyboard recognition gate: shots.md gives every shot a "Recognisable as" column, and the other vendor judges it blind from the board before any set is built. Weak shots are redesigned first, preferably with real material that shows people (photos, archival or modern footage). The cant-escape-ai-2 board had 15 of 32 shots rated weak before anything was rendered.  (from cant-escape-ai-2 2026-10-08)
- R49 [manual] The Blender MCP is for single stills and quick probes: a multi-variant render in one call times out (the work continues in Blender; read its output file). Look-dev sheets go through lib.lookdev_sheet in one short call per set or run headless; batches always go through tools/render-shot.py.  (from cant-escape-ai-2 2026-10-08)
- R50 [manual] tools/render-shot.py renders incrementally (content hash of scene script, lib.py, table entry, frames and scale; --force overrides) and measures every clip's mid-frame mean luma, failing the batch outside 14-225; mid frames land in renders/mid/ for the contact sheet.  (from the v1 near-black shots, cant-escape-ai 2026-10-08)
- R51 [manual] Write every JSON and text data file with encoding="utf-8": a Windows-default write turned "·" into a cp1252 byte that crashed the render queue. Captions use the spelling the speech recognizer hears ("kilometers", not "kilometres"), or caption alignment fails.  (from cant-escape-ai-2 2026-10-08)
- R52 [manual] Machine learning shown on screen is real: detection boxes come from the model run on that footage (tools/detect.py, OpenCV YuNet), and no shot draws a match, identity or score the model did not produce.  (from cant-escape-ai-2 2026-10-08)
- R53 [manual] A human voiceover goes through tools/ingest-voice.py: takes in script order, aligned to the script by word (difflib), one WAV per line cut at the quietest point of each gap, words.json written from the script's own words, every line end verified by re-transcription; never give Whisper the script as a prompt (it skips sentences it recognises). Wording the recognizer cannot settle (two phrases that sound alike) is written as in the script and reported to the user, since an agent cannot hear. A quotation shown on screen stays verbatim: if the reading adds a word, show it as a paraphrase without quotation marks or have the phrase re-recorded.  (from cant-escape-ai-2 2026-10-11)
- R54 [manual] A looping short ends on a sentence that leads into its first line, with a head of a few frames and no tail, no end card and no ringing last hit; the score returns to its opening level.  (from cant-escape-ai-2 2026-10-11)
