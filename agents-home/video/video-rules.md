# Video rules (read at every stage; retrospectives add to the end)

- Never start production before the user approves the definition of good, a style frame and the animatic.  (from agi-race 2026-09-29)
- No slideshow pacing: every shot has motion inside it and a purpose; cuts land on the beat.  (from agi-race 2026-09-29)
- No generic generated imagery as the default: generative engines are opt-in and must pass the anti-slop gate.  (from agi-race 2026-09-29)
- Anti-slop gate: reject generic stock look, visuals that do not carry their line, AI artifacts, style drift, motion that goes nowhere.  (from agi-race 2026-09-29)
- The voice is part of the style lock: send a short audition of 3-4 voices with the style frame, because no agent can judge a voice by ear; never ship a voice the user has not heard.  (from agi-race-2 2026-10-01)
- Freeze line slots in beats once picture work starts; a new voice must fit the slots, not move the cuts.  (from agi-race-2 2026-10-01)
- Review every delegated shot on a contact sheet (first, middle, last frame) before merging its look into the film; a passing verify only proves it renders.  (from agi-race-2 2026-10-01)
- A tool or service is tested with one real call when it is added; a connector that needs credits is a paid dependency.  (from agi-race-2 2026-10-01)
- Research: open the page for every claim in the script; search-result summaries are leads, not sources.  (from agi-race-2 2026-10-01)
- Validate media roots before any write and keep each version's rendered public assets separate; sharing a source or module junction must not share writable output. (from alignment v2 2026-10-02)
- Run frozen-frame checks on the assembled timeline as well as suspect clips: a transition's held destination plus the following shot's quiet opening can fail across the cut. Never waive the detector for an unintended hold. (from alignment v2 2026-10-02)
- Transition source scenes must omit captions that would shrink or be wiped into fragments; inspect first, middle and last transition frames at full resolution. (from alignment v2 2026-10-02)
- Authored asset files are only a starting point for craft. Check silhouette, surface, articulated contact, readable framing and visual meaning in the rendered result. (from alignment v2 2026-10-02)
- Distinguish reviewer evidence: a midpoint cannot establish that an answer never appears, a contact sheet cannot establish playback or sound, and unread sources cannot establish traceability. Preserve each independent verdict and its unknowns. (from alignment v2 2026-10-02)
- Set PYTHONUTF8=1 and PYTHONIOENCODING=utf-8 for media CLIs on Windows. Treat a transcript sentence over a verified silent voice tail as a recognizer error, not additional narration. (from alignment v2 2026-10-02)
- Serialize verification runs that share a bundle folder. Parallel inspection is safe; rebundling while another render reads that folder is not. (from alignment v2 2026-10-02)
